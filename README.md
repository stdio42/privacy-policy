# stdio42 Privacy Policy

**Last updated: December 30, 2025**

## Our Philosophy

We build apps that respect you. No tracking. No ads. No data collection. No nonsense.

We're tired of software that treats users as products. Our apps do one thing and do it well — without compromising your privacy.

---

## Privacy Policy

### The Short Version

**We don't collect your data. Period.**

Our apps run entirely on your device. We don't have servers collecting your information. We don't track you. We don't serve ads. We don't sell anything about you because we don't know anything about you.

### What We Don't Collect

- Personal information (name, email, phone number)
- Location data
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Device identifiers
- Browsing or viewing history
- Contact lists
- Photos or media (unless stored locally by the app for offline use, and only on your device)
- Any data for advertising purposes

### What Stays on Your Device

Some of our apps store data locally on your device to function properly:

- **Server configurations** — If you configure a server URL (e.g., for Jellyfin), this is stored locally on your device only.
- **Cached content** — Downloaded media for offline viewing stays on your device. We never upload it anywhere.
- **Preferences** — Your app settings are stored locally.

This data never leaves your device. We can't access it. We don't want to.

### Third-Party Services

Our apps may connect to servers you configure (such as your personal Jellyfin server). We don't control these servers and aren't responsible for their privacy practices. We simply provide a client to access your own content.

We do not integrate any:
- Analytics SDKs
- Advertising networks
- Crash reporting services that collect personal data
- Social media trackers
- Any third-party code that phones home

### Children's Privacy

We don't collect data from anyone, including children. Our apps are safe for users of all ages.

### Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we'll update this page. But honestly, our commitment to not collecting your data isn't going to change.

### Contact

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue at [github.com/stdio42](https://github.com/stdio42).
